From **6 to 9 October 2026** we will be at the **Global Heat Forum** in Bangkok, Thailand, the leading international gathering on heat, health and climate resilience. The Forum is convened by the Global Heat Health Information Network (GHHIN), Wellcome, the Rockefeller Foundation and ClimateWorks Foundation, and this year focuses on moving from evidence to action to scale heat resilience.

EscuelasCool will take part in several presentations and activities, sharing the work we are carrying out in Chile, Colombia and Peru to make the impact of extreme heat in schools visible.

We are very much looking forward to learning from what is happening in other regions of the world and bringing those experiences back to Latin America.
